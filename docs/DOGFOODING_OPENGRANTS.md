## Project Profile

- **ID**: `opengrants-os`
- **Name**: OpenGrants OS
- **GitHub**: https://github.com/ANAMIZED/opengrants-os
- **License**: Apache-2.0
- **Focus areas**: open-source, artificial intelligence, public-goods, MCP, agentic AI, funding infrastructure, open-weight models

**Description**  
OpenGrants OS is an open-source Autonomous Agentic AI MCP Server that provides end-to-end grants discovery, ranking, profile matching, proposal drafting, and lifecycle management. It is optimized for open-source AI and public-goods work, and uniquely treats traditional government grants and donation/sponsorship/quadratic public-goods funding as equal first-class citizens. Built to eliminate the discovery tax that disadvantages independent open-source AI maintainers relative to well-resourced closed labs.

## Full Proposal Outline  
**(Opportunity: GitHub Sponsors + Open Collective Public-Goods Support)**

### 1. Executive Summary / Abstract
Summarize OpenGrants OS and its alignment with sustainable public-goods funding. Emphasize open-source outputs, the discovery tax problem, and the unique inclusion of donation/sponsorship vehicles alongside traditional grants.

### 2. Why This Public-Goods Funding Vehicle
Explain why ongoing sponsorship and collective funding (rather than one-off traditional grants) is the right model for infrastructure that must continuously track funding sources, maintain ranking models, and serve the community. Commit to transparency via Open Collective and public development on GitHub.

### 3. Problem Statement & Motivation
Independent open-source AI maintainers face a severe information and capacity asymmetry versus well-resourced closed labs when seeking funding. Existing tools are either closed SaaS or fragmented across dozens of portals. OpenGrants OS exists to close that gap as public infrastructure.

### 4. Project Description & Approach
- Autonomous MCP Server surface (usable by any agent)
- Multi-source discovery (Grants.gov, NSF, public-goods catalogs, extensible)
- Profile Steward for personalized matching
- Multi-agent ranking with open-source bias
- Grounded proposal drafting (adapts to grant vs. donation vehicles)
- Continuous ingestion + evaluation harness
- Fully open-source (Apache-2.0), self-hostable

### 5. Open-Source & Public-Goods Commitment
All code, schemas, evaluation data, and documentation remain Apache-2.0. No proprietary lock-in. Community contributions welcomed. The system itself is designed so other public-goods projects can dogfood it.

### 6. Team & Prior Work
Built in the open on GitHub. Early milestones already delivered: live Grants.gov + NSF + public-goods discovery, MCP interface, profile + drafting agents, evaluation harness, and continuous ingestion scaffolding.

### 7. Impact & Evaluation
Success metrics:
- Adoption by open-source AI maintainers and research groups
- Number of opportunities surfaced that would otherwise be missed
- Quality of drafted materials (human preference / downstream success)
- Community contributions and extensions of the data sources
- Reduction in time spent on pure discovery by maintainers

### 8. Timeline & Budget Narrative
Sponsorship / collective funding will support:
- Expansion of data sources (NIH, Horizon Europe, more foundations)
- Production hardening and self-hosting documentation
- Richer multi-agent drafting and red-teaming
- Evaluation datasets and public benchmarks
- Community support and onboarding materials
